Frequently asked questions

What is Cartlight: ChatGPT Ads HQ?

Cartlight: ChatGPT Ads HQ is a Shopify app from Cartlight Labs. It checks whether your catalog and margins fit ChatGPT Ads, catches feed and conversion-tracking issues in plain language, and tracks your true profit per campaign and product.

Do I need a ChatGPT Ads account first?

No for getting started: you can install Cartlight and run the free Fit Check using your Shopify store data alone. You will need a ChatGPT Ads account and API key when you are ready to sync live campaigns, tracking, and profit reports.

Where do I find my ChatGPT Ads API key?

In ChatGPT Ads Manager, open Settings, create an API key for your ad account, then paste it into Cartlight. Step-by-step screenshots are coming soon.

Is my API key safe?

Yes. Your API key is encrypted at rest, never shown in full after you enter it, and used only to run the features you enable. You can disconnect it in Cartlight at any time or revoke it in your ads account.

Why aren't my products showing in ChatGPT Ads?

Common culprits include missing GTINs or barcodes, price or stock mismatches between Shopify and your product feed, rejected product images, or out-of-stock items. Cartlight's Feed Doctor flags each affected product and tells you what to fix.

How is "true profit" calculated?

True profit is revenue minus product cost, shipping, payment and platform fees, and ad spend. Product cost is pulled from the "cost per item" field in Shopify, and Cartlight flags any products missing a cost so your totals stay accurate.

Will Cartlight pause my ads automatically?

Only if you turn on rules. By default, Cartlight only sends alerts. When you enable automated spend protection rules, every action is logged and can be undone.

Does Cartlight work with the official ChatGPT Ads app?

Yes, it works alongside it. You can keep using the official app for campaign setup while Cartlight monitors catalog health, tracking reliability, and true profit.

Which countries are supported?

Cartlight works with Shopify stores worldwide. Live campaign syncing depends on where OpenAI supports ChatGPT Ads, and you can still run our free Fit Check from any country.

How do I cancel?

Uninstall Cartlight from your Shopify admin or downgrade to Free inside the app at any time. All billing is handled through Shopify.

Are you affiliated with OpenAI or Shopify?

No, Cartlight Labs is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI or Shopify.
