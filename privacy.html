Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Cartlight Labs ("Cartlight", "we", "us") builds apps for Shopify merchants, starting with Cartlight: ChatGPT Ads HQ. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have. If anything is unclear, email hello@cartlightlabs.com.

Operator: Cartlight Labs, [add your legal name and postal address here].

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers visitors to cartlightlabs.com, people who join our early-access waitlist, people who request a script tag check, and merchants who install our Shopify apps.

2. Information we collect

Waitlist and contact details. If you join early access or email us, we collect your email address, and optionally your Shopify store URL and whether you run ChatGPT Ads. We use this only to contact you about Cartlight.

Migration free-check form. If you request a free script tag check at /script-tag-migration, we collect your Shopify store URL, email address, your optional answer on custom developer code, and campaign referral parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, and utm_campaign). We use this to review your storefront, prepare and send your migration report and fixed-price quote, and see which channels bring merchants to us. Form submissions are processed and stored via Netlify Forms. To request deletion of your submission, email hello@cartlightlabs.com.

Shopify store data. When you install our app and approve its permissions, we access store details, products, product costs, and order data. We use this to calculate profit, check your catalog and tracking, and show reports to you.

ChatGPT Ads API keys. If you connect your ChatGPT Ads account, you give us an OpenAI Ads API key. We store it encrypted at rest and never display it in full after you enter it. You can disconnect it at any time, and you can revoke it in your ads account.

Data exchanged with ChatGPT Ads. At your request, we send data to and receive data from ChatGPT Ads on your behalf, such as product feed information, purchase conversion events, and campaign performance figures (spend, clicks, conversions). Without your connection, we do not send anything.

Website usage. We may use privacy-friendly analytics that do not use advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, secure and improve our apps and website.
  • To calculate and display your reports, alerts and recommendations.
  • To send service messages and, if you opted in, product updates.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your data or your customers' data, and we do not use it for advertising to others.

5. Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run Cartlight (for example hosting, database, and email delivery), with Shopify and OpenAI as part of the connections you authorize, and when required by law. Our providers may process data only to perform services for us.

6. Shopify customer data requests

Our apps respond to Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks: customer data requests, customer data erasure, and shop data erasure. When Shopify sends one of these, we process it within the timeframes Shopify requires.

7. Retention and deletion

If you uninstall our app, we stop accessing your store, and we delete your store data and stored API keys, in line with Shopify's data-erasure process. Waitlist emails are kept until you ask us to delete them or unsubscribe. Limited records may be kept where the law requires it.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, encryption at rest for API keys, and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you about incidents as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To use these rights, email hello@cartlightlabs.com.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 16.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and notify merchants of material changes.

13. Contact

Cartlight Labs — hello@cartlightlabs.com
